Our Mission is to transport you safely.

Problem Statement

Rural Mobility Left Behind.

Many rural and underserved parts of Ghana lack access to mainstream mobility services. Unregulated motorbike taxis, locally called Okada, create accidents, unclear prices, and limited access to basic services, leaving communities isolated and economically stagnant.

Our Solution

Building for People, not Cars

ORDA Rides makes rural travel safe and reliable by partnering with okada riders and local businesses. Verified profiles, safety training, and clear pricing build trust and turn informal work into dignified livelihoods.

Our Vision

Bringing Rural Ghana Onboard

We envision a Ghana where rural communities are fully included in the mobility economy. ORDA RIDES exists to make safe, reliable, and dignified transportation accessible to every town, no matter how small.

Our Impact

Change is already here.

Since launching in our pilot community of Adeiso, Upper West Akim District - E/R, we have onboarded over 250 users, with 92+ active users relying on the platform for their daily needs everyday. By training and retraining over 20 riders, we have established a formal safety standard that is currently changing lives.
The future is only brighter!
